# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in contributing! We welcome contributions of all kinds and ask that you follow these guidelines to ensure smooth collaboration.

---

## 🐛 Reporting Bugs

If you find a bug:

- Open an [issue](../../issues) in the repository.  
- Include a **clear description** and **steps to reproduce** the issue.  

---

## 💡 Suggesting Enhancements

We welcome feature requests and ideas!  
Please open an [issue](../../issues) and describe your proposed enhancement in detail.
